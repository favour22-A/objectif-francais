Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Objectif Français is a French vocabulary app. This page explains what data the app keeps and why. Short version: only what is needed to save your progress. No ads, and your data is never sold.

What we store

  • Account: your email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email and basic profile from Google; we do not get your Google password or access to your Google data.
  • Learning progress: which words you rated as known, familiar or not known, and when.
  • Settings: your learning goal, French variety and audio preferences.
  • Contributions (contributors only): dictionary words and corrections you add, and word problem reports you submit.

Guest accounts store progress without an email address.

What we do not store

  • Texts you paste into the text analyzer stay in your browser and are not saved.
  • No advertising or tracking cookies.

Services we use

  • Supabase: accounts and database.
  • Vercel: hosts the website.
  • Google: optional sign-in.
  • MyMemory translation: when a word has no built-in translation, that single word is sent to get one.

Your browser also keeps a local copy of your progress so the app works quickly and offline.

Deleting your data

Email cibitoru@gmail.com to have your account and progress deleted. We will do it within 30 days.
